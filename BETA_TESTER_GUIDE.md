# STRATA Beta Tester Quick-Start Guide

Welcome to the STRATA beta. Follow these steps to test a clean client model from start to finish:

1. **Sign In & Start Fresh:** Open the app, sign in on the **Home Portal**, and click **Start New Blank Project** (optionally entering a client name first) so you begin with a clean slate and no leftover test data.
2. **Configure & Enter Data:**
   * Use **Data Ingestion Gateway** to upload a PDF, CSV, image, or text file, review items in the scratchpad queue, and commit them.
   * Use **Data Input Parameters** and **Data Entry Panel** to add or adjust sales, COGS, overheads, payroll, CapEx, and equity funding, then click **Save Current** / **Persist Current Working State to Disk**.
3. **Review Statements & Exports:** Open **Performance Tab**, choose your forecasting horizon in years, check the Profit & Loss, Cash Flow, and Balance Sheet tabs (including the trial balance checksum), and test the CSV or PDF exports.
4. **Share Feedback:** Note which project/scenario you were testing, what action you took, what you expected vs. what happened, and include a screenshot if something looks off.
